About

I am a PhD candidate under the supervision of Prof. Lawrence H. Le at the University of Alberta (expected completion May 2027). My dissertation, "Advancing Dental Imaging: 3D Reconstruction of Intraoral Structures using High-frequency Ultrasound with Machine Learning," combines freehand 3D ultrasound, deep learning, and segmentation to make radiation-free periodontal assessment practical.

Before Alberta, I completed a first-class Honours BSc in Computer Science at Eastern University, Sri Lanka, and worked on tractography and brain connectivity analysis. I also collaborate on pediatric inflammatory bowel disease ultrasound, carotid ultrasound reconstruction, and quantum-inspired models for Alzheimer's disease.

Research

Freehand 3D intraoral ultrasound

A portable system for real-time 3D reconstruction of intraoral structures, validated on phantoms and ex-vivo models.

Periodontal analysis in ultrasound video

Landmark detection, one-shot segmentation, and automatic assessment of the periodontium (DetSegDiff, OralSAM).

Pediatric IBD ultrasound

AI-assisted bowel wall thickness measurement and texture analysis, expanding to a multi-centre collaboration across Alberta and British Columbia.

Neural implicit reconstruction

Geometric-constraint models for surface reconstruction of freehand 3D ultrasound, including carotid imaging.

Brain connectivity and quantum ML

Graph attention networks and quantum-inspired transformers for Alzheimer's disease and neurological classification.

Living Muscle Twin

Machine learning with quantitative ultrasound for an AI-driven digital twin predicting muscle health.
