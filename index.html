About

Turning ultrasound into 3D insight

I'm a PhD candidate in Medical Sciences at the University of Alberta, supervised by Prof. Lawrence H. Le. My dissertation develops freehand 3D reconstruction of intraoral structures with high-frequency ultrasound and machine learning, aiming for safe, portable alternatives to X-ray in periodontal care.

My work spans deep learning, computer vision, and clinical collaboration, from foundation-model segmentation (OralSAM) and diffusion-based landmark detection (DetSegDiff) to pediatric bowel ultrasound and neural implicit surfaces.

Research

What I'm working on

Freehand 3D intraoral ultrasound

A compact, real-time system that reconstructs teeth and surrounding bone in 3D without radiation, validated on phantoms and ex-vivo models.

AI for periodontal analysis

OralSAM and DetSegDiff automatically segment and measure periodontal structures in ultrasound video.

Pediatric bowel ultrasound

AI-assisted wall-thickness measurement and texture analysis for inflammatory bowel disease, moving toward point-of-care use.

Neural surfaces and brain networks

Implicit surface reconstruction of 3D ultrasound, plus graph and quantum-inspired models for neurological disease.
