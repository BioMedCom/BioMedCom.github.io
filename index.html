About Me

Biography & Core Interests

I am a PhD Candidate in Medical Sciences within the Department of Radiology and Diagnostic Imaging at the University of Alberta, working under the guidance of Prof. Lawrence H. Le. My doctoral dissertation, "Advancing Dental Imaging: 3D Reconstruction of Intraoral Structures using High-frequency Ultrasound with Machine Learning", tackles the fundamental challenges of radiation-free 3D intraoral imaging for periodontal and orthodontic diagnosis.

Prior to joining the University of Alberta, I graduated with a First-Class Honours BSc in Computer Science (GPA 3.81/4.00) from Eastern University, Sri Lanka, where I developed deep learning algorithms on 3D curves for whole-brain white matter tractography. Following my undergraduate degree, I served as a Lecturer in Information Technology at Eastern University and an Instructor in Computer Science at the University of Jaffna.

My current research bridges medical ultrasonics, computer vision, and machine learning: developing real-time freehand 3D ultrasound reconstruction systems, foundation-model adaptions for ultrasound segmentation (such as OralSAM), diffusion-based landmark detection (DetSegDiff), and extending AI ultrasound analytics to pediatric inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), carotid neural implicit representations, and quantum-classical models for Alzheimer's disease.

Research Highlights

Core themes in medical imaging & applied artificial intelligence
Lead Researcher

Freehand 3D Intraoral Ultrasound

Engineering a compact, real-time 3D freehand ultrasound reconstruction system for dental and periodontal scanning. Incorporates sensor tracking, volume compounding, and spatial calibration validated across tissue-mimicking phantoms and pre-clinical ex-vivo porcine models.

IEEE TIM 2026 IEEE IUS 3D Volumetric Imaging
Lead Developer

OralSAM & DetSegDiff: Periodontal AI

Developed OralSAM, a one-shot segmentation framework for intraoral ultrasound videos utilizing adaptive feature correlation and self-prompting strategies, alongside DetSegDiff, an edge-enhanced diffusion network for simultaneous landmark detection and periodontium boundary segmentation.

JDR 2025 MICCAI 2025 Comp. Bio. Med. 2024
Co-Investigator

Pediatric Intestinal Ultrasound & SmartIUS

AI-assisted automatic bowel wall thickness measurement and multi-scale texture classification for pediatric Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD). Powering the SmartIUS handheld initiative spanning collaborative validation across the University of Alberta, UBC, and the University of Calgary.

J. Crohn's & Colitis (IF: 10.3) BSPC Point-of-Care
Co-First Author

Neural Implicit Surface Reconstruction

Formulated geometric-constraint neural implicit representations using unsigned distance functions (UDF) to reconstruct smooth, continuous 3D surfaces from sparse and noisy freehand ultrasound volumes, applied to carotid artery and musculoskeletal anatomy.

Medical Image Analysis (IF: 11.8) Neural Surfaces
Lead Author

Quantum ML & Brain Connectivity

Investigating hybrid quantum-classical vision transformers with variational quantum circuits (VQC) and explainable Graph Attention Networks (GAT) to model structural brain connectivity networks for early diagnosis of Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease.

IEEE JBHI (Under Review) Qiskit & PennyLane Graph ML
Research Coordinator

The Living Muscle Twin

Constructing an AI-driven dynamic digital twin that integrates quantitative ultrasound (QUS) biomarkers with patient physiological metrics to predict longitudinal muscle health, sarcopenia progression, and body composition changes.

NFRF Exploration Digital Twin Quantitative Ultrasound
